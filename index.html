You don't need a math degree, a fancy laptop, or years of programming to begin. You need a clear path and steady, small steps. Let's build that path together.

The Big Picture — What These Words Actually Mean

Before the roadmap, let's untangle four words people use interchangeably. They're like nested boxes — each one fits inside the last.

The Big Box

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Any technique that makes a computer do something "smart" — from a chess engine to a chatbot.

Inside AI

Machine Learning (ML)

Instead of hand-writing rules, you let the computer learn patterns from data. This is where most real work lives.

Inside ML

Deep Learning (DL)

ML using "neural networks" with many layers. Powers image recognition, speech, and modern language models.

The Hot Topic

Generative AI & LLMs

Models like Claude or ChatGPT that create text, images, and code. Built on deep learning, trained on huge datasets.

Your 6-Phase Roadmap

You don't have to do all of this, and you definitely don't have to do it in a rush. Think of these as checkpoints. Most beginners spend a few weeks per phase. Pick the depth that matches your goal — curiosity, career, or building a product.

Start Here This Week — 4 Concrete Actions

  1. Install Python & open a notebook. Download Anaconda or just use Google Colab in your browser (zero setup).
  2. Spend 2–3 hours on Python basics. Variables, lists, loops, functions. That's plenty for now.
  3. Play with an AI tool daily. Ask Claude or ChatGPT to explain one concept from this roadmap in simple terms.
  4. Pick your "why." Career? Building a product? Curiosity? Write it down — it keeps you going on hard days.
